Commissions

How a commission runs.

Consult

We visit the space or work from your photographs, then agree on scale, palette, and budget before anything is cut.

Compose

The piece is built in the atelier from that week's market and our own growers. You approve a study before we finish.

Install

We deliver, place, and light the work ourselves, and we return to strike it when the flowers are done.
